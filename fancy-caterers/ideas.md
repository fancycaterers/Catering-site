# FANCY CATERERS — Design Direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Deccan Heritage Table
Very Brief Intro: A warm editorial identity rooted in Hyderabad’s culinary heritage, balancing refined hospitality with the richness of shared meals. Photography, deep spice tones, and quiet ceremonial details make the legacy feel lived-in rather than nostalgic.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Ivory Banquet Studio
Very Brief Intro: A light, gallery-like direction with generous whitespace, crisp food photography, and a premium event-studio sensibility. It feels clean, modern, and highly customizable for weddings, corporate events, and private gatherings.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Midnight Feast
Very Brief Intro: A dramatic evening-led identity using charcoal surfaces, saffron light, and jewel-toned food imagery to evoke grand celebrations after dark. It is expressive and cinematic, but deliberately reserved for this one direction.
Probability: 0.02

## Selected approach: Deccan Heritage Table

### Design Movement
Contemporary Indian editorial design with references to Deccan craft, hand-printed menus, brass serviceware, and the pacing of a beautifully hosted meal. The design should feel established and tactile without looking ornamental or dated.

### Core Principles
1. Make the 21-year legacy visible through confident editorial hierarchy, not through clutter or excessive decoration.
2. Let food photography carry appetite and credibility while the interface stays calm, legible, and conversion-focused.
3. Use asymmetry and offset compositions to suggest a hosted table setting rather than a generic centered landing page.
4. Keep every contact action immediate: call, WhatsApp, and request-a-quote should be obvious on both mobile and desktop.

### Color Philosophy
The palette uses a roasted-clove brown as the grounding neutral, turmeric gold as an ownable accent, and soft rice-ivory as the breathing space. The colors are meant to suggest warmth, freshness, and generous hospitality. Green appears sparingly as a cue for ingredient quality and vegetarian options, while a muted chili red is reserved for small moments of emphasis.

### Layout Paradigm
A long-form editorial landing page that alternates between full-bleed photography, offset text blocks, and narrow “menu card” bands. Sections should feel like chapters in a printed catering portfolio: legacy, cuisine, occasions, gallery, and quote request. Avoid over-centering; use a strong left rail for labels and a wide right field for content.

### Signature Elements
- A slim vertical “Since 2005” heritage rail that reappears at major section transitions.
- Small saffron rule marks and brass-like hairline borders inspired by menu printing and serving trays.
- A floating WhatsApp quote button that remains useful without obscuring content.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like being personally guided by a host: clear, warm, and responsive. Hover states reveal useful context rather than decorative noise. The quote flow should reduce uncertainty by explaining that the team will ask for event requirements and return a reasonable quote.

### Animation
Use short, physical transitions under 300ms with a confident ease-out. Hero text enters with a subtle upward reveal, gallery cards lift by a few pixels on hover, and section labels fade in with small staggered timing. Avoid looping motion or flashy effects. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep buttons immediately responsive with a small active-scale confirmation.

### Typography System
Use `DM Serif Display` for editorial headlines and `Manrope` for body copy, labels, navigation, and form controls. Headlines should be high-contrast and slightly expressive; body copy should remain compact and highly readable. Use uppercase letter-spaced micro-labels for section metadata, with sentence-case CTAs that sound human.

### Brand Essence
FANCY CATERERS brings 21 years of Hyderabad hospitality to weddings, family celebrations, corporate gatherings, and events across Telangana — with generous food, clean execution, and fair quotes.
Personality adjectives: generous, grounded, assured.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should sound appetizing and assured. CTAs should be direct but personal. Microcopy should remove friction and explain what happens next instead of making vague promises.

Example line 1: “A table worth gathering around.”

Example line 2: “Tell us the occasion. We’ll take care of the spread.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact emblem built from a stylized serving cloche and a four-point Deccan star, drawn as a bold single-color mark with a small cut-out highlight. Pair it with a custom-feeling uppercase wordmark set in a high-contrast serif, with “CATERERS” tracked below as a quiet descriptor. The mark should work independently as the favicon and mobile header symbol.

### Signature Brand Color
Turmeric Brass — `#C8872D`. It should appear as a recognizable accent on rules, buttons, highlights, and the emblem, always balanced by roasted-clove brown and rice-ivory rather than used as a broad gradient.

## Implementation reminder
Every CSS, component, and page file should begin with a short comment reminding the editor that this file follows the Deccan Heritage Table direction: contemporary Indian editorial warmth, roasted-clove / turmeric-brass / rice-ivory palette, asymmetric hosted-table layouts, DM Serif Display + Manrope typography, and restrained tactile motion.
